# Content Studio – Documentation

Content Studio is a self-contained AI writing workspace. It runs as a single page and uses Claude to draft and refine content.

## Quick start
1. Choose a content type (or open **Templates**), or use a **Quick action**.
2. Describe what you need in the request box. Optionally set goal, audience, tone, length, language and creativity.
3. Select **Generate**. Your draft appears in the page preview.
4. Refine with the chips, a custom change, or edit the text directly.
5. Copy, print or download, or find it later in **History**.

## Features
- **Content types and templates** – many formats, each with a starting template.
- **Quick actions** – Improve writing, Rewrite, Summarize, Expand, Shorten, Humanize, Repurpose, Brainstorm. They pre-fill the request and keep text you already typed.
- **Improve my prompt** – turns a rough request into a clearer brief. You see the original and improved versions and choose Use improved prompt, Copy or Keep original.
- **Refinement** – Improve, Make concise, Humanize, Improve flow, Stronger intro and Stronger conclusion, plus earlier options and custom instructions. In Edit mode, select text to refine only that part; with no selection the whole document is refined.
- **Brand kit** – saved voice settings applied when "Use my brand kit" is ticked.
- **History, favorites, search** – previous generations are kept locally.
- **Preview and output** – page preview, inline editing, fullscreen, copy, print, download.
- **Theme and settings** – light/dark theme and autosave preference.

## Autosave and unsaved changes
- The draft (form and output) is saved shortly after you type. The status line shows *Saving…*, *Saved*, or a storage error.
- After a refresh, a banner offers to restore your draft.
- Editing any form field, the content type, a template or the output marks the document as unsaved. Starting a new document then asks: **Save & Continue**, **Discard Changes** or **Cancel**.

## Data and privacy
- **Stored in your browser:** drafts, history, favorites, brand kit and preferences.
- **Sent to Claude:** your request, the settings you chose, the brand kit if enabled, and any text you ask it to refine.
- **Not stored:** API keys or secrets. The page holds none.
- Clearing local preferences resets settings; it no longer duplicates the refine chips.

## Known limitations
Not yet available: conversational mode, repurposing hub, versions with comparison, projects, prompt library, SEO and quality analysis, and extra export formats (Markdown, HTML, JSON, DOCX).

## Troubleshooting
- **AI unavailable:** open the app from its claude.ai link.
- **Generation fails:** your existing text is kept; try again.
- **Storage full:** delete old history entries or export what you need.
